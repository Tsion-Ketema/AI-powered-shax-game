The game rule.

There are two players lets label them player1, player2.
Each of the players have 12 pieces of each.
The shax board have 24 postion that each players places there pieces.
mills-->3 similar pieces in a row.

The  game have 4 phases eachh takes place in step (One after the other).
1. Placement phases
2. Opening phases
3. Movement phase
4. Capture phase

1, Placement phase
    This is the phase where the game starts, each players place there pieces turn by turn in the 24 postions.
    During this time any of the player might form a mill.

    Condtions
        1. One of the player forms one or more mills.
        2. Both player forms 1 or more mill.
        3. None of the players forms a mill.

3. Movement phase
So after the opening phase is completed from all 24 pieces only 22 pieces left for further Movement
Condtions
1. Players take turn moving the pieces only in adjecent postion.
2. Diagonal moves and jumping is not allowed.
3. If a player forms a mill they remove their opponets piece.
4. If one of the player is blocked the opponet moves the his own pieces just to let him play (but can use this as an advantage for himself to make a mill only to help the opponet to give it a chance).its just to unblock
5. If a player only with 3 pieces it can fly (move to empty postion instead of the adjust postion)
6. The game ends when only 2 pieces are left
